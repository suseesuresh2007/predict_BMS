PREDICTIVE MONITORING

From Battery Data
To Early Warning

PredictBMS adds a predictive intelligence layer alongside the existing BMS to identify abnormal battery behavior before a critical threshold is reached.

  1. 01

    Sensors

    Continuous voltage, current, and temperature sensing from the battery system.

  2. 02

    Data

    Signals are organized into a live behavioral picture of the pack.

  3. 03

    Normal Behavior

    The system learns patterns associated with normal operating behavior.

  4. 04

    Anomaly Detection

    Deviations from the learned pattern are identified before a hard threshold alone drives the response.

  5. 05

    Risk Score

    Detected deviations are translated into a continuously updated battery risk score.

  6. 06

    Early Warning

    The dashboard can surface an early warning and represent a local buzzer or LED backup alert.

Sensor Layer

PredictBMS continuously observes voltage, current and temperature from the battery system.

AI Layer

The anomaly-detection layer learns patterns associated with normal battery behavior and identifies deviations from those patterns.

Risk Layer

Detected deviations are translated into a continuously updated battery risk score.

Alert Layer

When risk rises, the system provides an early warning through the dashboard and can trigger a local buzzer/LED alert.

THE PREDICTBMS PROTOTYPE

Predictive Safety
Starts With Better Signals

PredictBMS is designed around a simple idea: battery safety should not depend only on waiting for a fixed threshold to be crossed.

By continuously observing battery behavior and detecting deviations from normal patterns, PredictBMS aims to create an earlier window for inspection and intervention.

PROBLEM STATEMENT

Thresholds tell one part of the story.

India's rapidly growing EV fleet, particularly electric two-wheelers, has experienced battery-related fire incidents associated with thermal anomalies and battery safety concerns.

Existing BMS protection is primarily threshold-based. When a critical threshold is crossed, the available window for preventive action may already be limited.

PredictBMS addresses this gap with a low-cost, retrofit-friendly predictive layer designed to identify abnormal battery behavior earlier.
